# Optional paid help

Proofgate remains available under the MIT license. You can use it without buying help.

For a finite access-policy project, you can ask about paid help with modeling dimensions and invariants, reviewing semantic diffs, or planning an integration with the gateway. This is an inquiry route, not a promised service level, security certification or guarantee that a policy is safe.

Email [gerardrecinto@gmail.com](mailto:gerardrecinto@gmail.com) with your goals, a sanitized example, and any timing constraints. Please leave credentials, personal data and confidential production policies out of the initial message. Scope, availability and price are agreed separately before work starts.

For bugs and feature requests, use the public repository. For vulnerabilities, follow the [private reporting instructions](.github/SECURITY.md).
